## User

Act as an expert UX/UI designer and conversion copywriter. Design a responsive, high-converting desktop page for a lead magnet that gathers information such as name/phone number/email address in order to get a PDF called the "2026 Bathroom Budget Blueprint". The PDF will talk about setting realistic expectations, line items for a bathroom budget, design decisions that affect the price, how contractors price work, etc. 

Target Audience: My target audience is high income home owners that need functional changes to their bathroom for safety reasons OR they want an aesthetic makeover and are procrastinating to make one. 
Tone/Vibe: The user should feel like they can trust me and feel like this is a genuinely valuable, premium piece of an informational PDF. 

RULES & AESTHETICS (Ban the defaults):
- Typography: Use bold, distinctive typography. DO NOT use generic fonts like Inter, Arial, or Roboto. Pair a strong display font for headings with a clean, highly readable font for body copy.
- Colors: Create a strong, custom color palette with one dominant color, one sharp accent color, and high contrast. DO NOT use generic blue-to-purple gradients. 
- Layout: Incorporate at least one unexpected layout choice (e.g., asymmetry, overlapping elements, or a diagonal section) so the page feels custom-built, not AI-generated.
- Style: Use generous margins, whitespace, and subtle entrance animations/hover effects to make the page feel alive.

REQUIRED SECTIONS (in this order):
1. Navigation: Logo and one clear, high-contrast Call-to-Action (CTA) button.
2. Hero Section: Headline that speaks directly to the reader's pain point or desire (start with "You"), a compelling subheadline, a prominent CTA, and a small line of social proof (e.g., "Join X others").
3. Problem Agitation: Highlight the pain your reader feels before finding your solution.
4. Solution/Benefits: 3 to 4 key benefits (not features) written as clear outcomes.
5. Social Proof: 2 to 3 short testimonials or a credibility stat.
6. FAQ: 3 to 5 questions that handle common purchasing objections.
7. Final CTA: Repeat the offer and provide one last push.
8. Footer: Minimalist, clean links.
